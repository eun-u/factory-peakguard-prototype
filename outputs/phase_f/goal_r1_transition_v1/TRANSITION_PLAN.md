For each week and actual seed, fit a three-class LightGBM classifier for signed future change: fall < -30, neutral in [-30,30], rise > 30. Fit three separate class-conditional LightGBM L1 signed-change regressors. The probability-weighted class-median estimate plus observed origin power forms the expert forecast. It is an exploratory mixture, not a mathematically exact conditional mean, calibrated probability, or peak-event probability. Constrain signed predictions to class supports: fall <= -30, neutral clipped to [-30,30], rise >=30. Clip forecast power to zero. Neither true future class nor future production/weather may enter inference.

Preprocessing is FIT-only and common to all models. Require at least20 FIT and20 STOP rows per class, otherwise fail explicitly. No synthetic missing-class prediction or silent fallback.
